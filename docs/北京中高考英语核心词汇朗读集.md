## 使用与范围说明

- 每篇正文120–140个英文词（按空白分词，与网页统计一致），配22项目标词汇或短语及语境中文释义。
- 中考侧重日常生活、校园、基础叙事；高考侧重社会、科技、文化、研究与议论。学段和核心词汇由教学用途精选，未逐词标注官方课标归属，不是北京官方完整考试词表，也不宣称覆盖全部课标单词。
- 目标表达保留正文中的实际形式，例如 decided、took 或 caring for；同一词根的不同形式及跨篇复现不能理解为新增独立词根。
- 每篇先听读、再朗读、最后遮住英文用中文释义自测；配套三组理解问题和参考答案可用于口语复述。

### 01 · A Better Morning

主题：日常生活｜正文：127词｜目标词项：22

Every morning, I used to get up late and hurry to school without breakfast. I often felt tired during the first lesson and could hardly pay attention. Last Monday, I decided to change my habit. I prepared my schoolbag before going to bed and set an alarm beside my desk. When it rang, I stood up immediately instead of reaching for my phone. There was enough time to wash my face, enjoy a simple meal and check the weather. On the way to school, I noticed birds singing above the street. I arrived early and felt ready to learn. A healthy routine does not require a perfect plan. Start with one small step, keep trying, and a difficult morning may become the best part of your day.
